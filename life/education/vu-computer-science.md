# Computer Science at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam

> B.Sc. Computer Science | 2022 - present

## What changed

The degree gave structure to the programming projects that I began in 2019. It connected practical programming with systems, theory, and collaborative engineering.

I became especially interested in operating systems, infrastructure, programming languages, and the boundaries between software components.

## Academic work

My coursework includes operating systems, web technology, systems programming, and programming language concepts.

I earned 10/10 for a Philosophy of Mind paper about whether language models genuinely think. The paper argued that language can encode knowledge for rational inference.

## What the degree added

University gave me more than technical material. It also created the opportunity to teach, explain difficult concepts, and observe where explanations fail.

That teaching work became its own branch in the story. It later influenced how I communicate with customers, colleagues, and technical systems.

## Current state

The degree remains in progress. I continue to combine study with full-time software work and independent projects.
