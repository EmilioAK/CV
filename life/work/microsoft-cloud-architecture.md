# Cloud Architecture at Microsoft

> Cloud Solution Architect Intern | Amsterdam | September 2025 - February 2026

## Starting at Microsoft

I joined Microsoft as a Cloud Solution Architect Intern. The role combined customer discovery, architecture, implementation, and clear technical communication.

## Finding the integration gap

A customer needed to connect an external AI agent with Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365. The available path did not cover the complete requirement.

## Building for reuse

I designed and shipped a reusable proxy-agent architecture for the first customer. The result led to reuse across two additional customer engagements.

I became the primary technical contact for the follow-on work. My responsibility covered discovery, requirements, architecture, and implementation.

I packaged the approach for internal use. The package gave colleagues a reusable way to connect other external agents.

## What changed

The internship connected technical design with customer communication. A correct architecture was not enough by itself.

The work also required clear requirements, trust, and a reusable path for other people.
