# Teaching Computer Science

> Teaching Assistant | Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam | 2024 - 2025

I supported Operating Systems, Web Technology, and the Systems Programming Project. I led Web Technology classes of 30 students and supported an Operating Systems course with more than 400 students.

I rewrote parts of the Systems Programming Project manual. The instructors kept the revisions for later cohorts. I also built a Docker environment that gave macOS students a consistent setup for systems projects.

Teaching made clear that a correct answer is not enough. The explanation and the tooling must also remove the right obstacles.
